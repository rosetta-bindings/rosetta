\section{The kinds}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{longtable}{@{}lL{2.0cm}L{5.7cm}L{4.9cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Kind} & \textbf{Applies to} & \textbf{Inline / side-car} &
  \textbf{Meaning} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\code{doc} & fields, methods, classes$^*$ &
  \code{= rosetta::doc\{"\ldots"\}} \newline \code{"doc": "\ldots"} &
  Description text. \\
\addlinespace
\code{range} & numeric fields &
  \code{= rosetta::range\{lo, hi\}} \newline \code{"range": [lo, hi]} &
  Value bounds; validating setter, clamping slider, documented constraint. \\
\addlinespace
\code{readonly} & fields &
  \code{= rosetta::readonly} \newline \code{"readonly": true} &
  Getter-only property. \\
\addlinespace
\code{combobox} & string fields &
  \code{= rosetta::combobox\{\{"a","b"\}\}} \newline
  \code{"combobox": ["a","b"]} &
  Allowed choices, maximum 16. \\
\addlinespace
\code{label} & fields, methods &
  \code{= rosetta::label\{"\ldots"\}} \newline \code{"label": "\ldots"} &
  Display-name override in UI backends only. \\
\addlinespace
\code{button} & methods &
  \code{= rosetta::button\{"Run"\}} \newline \code{"button": "Run"} &
  Call-button caption. \\
\addlinespace
\code{widget::*} & fields &
  \code{= rosetta::widget::slider} \newline \code{"widget": "slider"} &
  Editor hint; see below. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\end{center}

\noindent$^*$ Class-level and free-function \code{doc} come from the manifest's
\field{doc} key, since free functions carry no in-source annotations.

\begin{center}

\begin{tabular}{@{}L{7.2cm}cc@{}}

 & \textbf{Inline} & \textbf{Side-car} \\
\midrule

Wiring a side-car:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=json]
"classes": [
  { "name": "Algo", "header": "Algo.h", "annotations": "Algo.ann.json" }

\section{Side-car schema}

Keys are member identifiers --- fields or methods. Each value is an object
carrying any subset of:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Key} & \textbf{JSON type} & \textbf{Example} \\
\midrule
\code{doc}      & string       & \code{"Visible items"} \\
\code{range}    & 2-element array & \code{[0, 100]}, \code{[1e-10, 1e-6]} \\
\code{readonly} & boolean      & \code{true} \\
\code{combobox} & string array & \code{["fast", "slow"]} \\
\code{label}    & string       & \code{"Max iterations"} \\
\code{button}   & string       & \code{"Run"} \\
\code{widget}   & string       & \code{"slider"} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

A key naming no member of the type \textbf{fails the build}, naming the
offending key. That is deliberate: a renamed field must not silently lose its
metadata.

\section{Merge rules}

\begin{itemize}
  \item No side-car: the class is un-annotated. Not an error.
  \item Inline annotations present: the JSON entries are \emph{concatenated}
    after them.
  \item Precedence: a reader that keeps the last match lets a JSON entry
    override an inline one of the same kind; cumulative readers such as
    \code{doc} see both.
\end{itemize}

\section{Internal}

\code{rosetta::virtual\_spec} is synthesised by the walk and never written by
users. It marks a method's reflection as virtual or overriding so backends can
emit trampolines.
