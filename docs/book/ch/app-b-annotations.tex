An annotation a backend has no use for is ignored, never an error.

\section{Side-car schema}

Keys are member identifiers --- fields or methods. Each value is an object
carrying any subset of:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{6.0cm}L{3.6cm}L{3.8cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Key} & \textbf{JSON type} & \textbf{Example} \\
\midrule
\code{doc}      & string       & \code{"Visible items"} \\
\code{range}    & 2-element array & \code{[0, 100]}, \code{[1e-10, 1e-6]} \\
\code{readonly} & boolean      & \code{true} \\
\code{combobox} & string array & \code{["fast", "slow"]} \\
\code{label}    & string       & \code{"Max iterations"} \\
\code{button}   & string       & \code{"Run"} \\
\code{widget}   & string       & \code{"slider"} \\
\code{unit}, \code{group}, \code{since}, \code{regex}, \code{confirm},
  \code{enabled\_if}, \code{visible\_if} & string & \code{"m"} \\
\code{min}, \code{max}, \code{step}, \code{decimals}, \code{order} & number &
  \code{0.1} \\
\code{length} & 2-element array & \code{[1, 64]} \\
\code{requires} & string, or [expr, message] & \code{["dt > 0", "why"]} \\
\code{destructive}, \code{expensive}, \code{hidden}, \code{log\_scale} &
  boolean & \code{true} \\
\code{deprecated} & boolean or string & \code{"use foo()"} \\
\code{default} & object & \code{\{"dt": 0.001\}} \\
\code{example} & any JSON value & \code{\{"seconds": 2\}} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

A key naming no member of the type \textbf{fails the build}, naming the
offending key. That is deliberate: a renamed field must not silently lose its
metadata.

\section{Merge rules}

\begin{itemize}
  \item No side-car: the class is un-annotated. Not an error.
  \item Inline annotations present: the JSON entries are \emph{concatenated}
    after them.
  \item Precedence: a reader that keeps the last match lets a JSON entry
    override an inline one of the same kind; cumulative readers such as
    \code{doc} see both.
\end{itemize}

\section{Internal}

\code{rosetta::virtual\_spec} is synthesised by the walk and never written by
users. It marks a method's reflection as virtual or overriding so backends can
emit trampolines.
